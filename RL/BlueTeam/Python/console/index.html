Train the directional warning-time policy against an inference-only Red scenario generator—not a pretrained Red model. Every episode samples five distinct, reproducible approach sectors from an eight-sector benchmark at the same fixed spawn radius, then Unreal flies the drones toward the target. This tab temporarily owns the Unreal bridge.

Completed runs add the validation-selected policy and exact highest-warning sampled episode to Compare placements separately. Stop retains recordings and checkpoints without running a new final test or publishing a model.

Choose how complete native episode returns update the masked placement policy.

The selected layout gives the policy a starting preference. Training also explores other legal placements.

Sensor count is exact: every training and held-out layout uses that many legal sensors. Native episodes can be slow; the 10,000-episode ceiling prevents accidental unbounded runs, and Stop safely remains available.

Training and its contractor comparison use only the selected realistic, limited-FOV sensor profiles. Both use the same sensor selection, count and budget.

Exploration and evaluation

For common-sense starts, 0.2 gives the first placement decision a 20% chance of choosing outside the starting layout. Later probabilities change with legal choices and learning.

The baseline and each evaluated policy face the same validation scenarios. A separate final test checks unseen scenarios. Episode replays and logs are retained between policy snapshots.
